무엇이든 떨어지기 전에 빨간 예고 표시가 뜹니다. 보고, 판단하고, 피하면 됩니다. 맵마다 식칼, 고드름, 화물 상자, 포크가 떨어지고, 시간이 갈수록 더 자주 떨어집니다. 20초가 지나면 양옆에서 칼·눈덩이·나무 상자·마카롱이 일자로 날아옵니다. 머리 높이로 오면 엎드리고, 낮게 오면 점프하세요.

피하기만 하는 게임은 아닙니다. 대시로 부딪히면 상대가 밀려나 잠깐 납작하게 기절하고, 다른 젤리의 머리 위에 내려앉아도 기절시킬 수 있습니다. 상자를 밀어 길을 막고, 맵 가운데 점프 발판을 밀어 옮겨 높이 튀어 오르세요.

## English (en-US)

### Product name
Jelly Survival Party

### Short description (keep under 270 characters)
Become a tiny jelly and dodge the kitchen knives raining from above! Up to 8 players join online and the last one standing wins. Dash to shove, stomp heads to stun, and duck under knives flying in from the side.

### Description
Jelly Survival Party is a 2D online survival party game for up to 8 players. Play as a squishy jelly, jelly bear or pudding living in a giant kitchen, dodge the knives that drop from above, and hold out until you are the last one left.

Everything that falls is announced by a red warning marker first. Watch, decide, dodge. Each map drops its own hazard (kitchen knives, icicles, cargo crates, forks), faster as the round goes on, and after 20 seconds knives, snowballs, wooden crates and macarons fly straight in from the sides: duck when they come at head height, jump when they come low.

It is not only about dodging. Dash into someone to knock them back and flatten them for a moment, or land on another jelly's head to stun them. Push boxes to block the way, and shove the jump pad in the middle of the map to launch yourself high.

Four maps, each with its own twist:
- Kitchen: a three-level spice shelf tower and burners that catch fire mid-round
- Winter Eaves: slippery ice, snow awnings that collapse and come back, wind that pushes everyone one way
- Warehouse: a crane pallet sliding back and forth and a conveyor belt
- Dessert Shop: a cake lift that rises and sinks and a syrup floor that slows you down

The host picks the next map and how maps change (in order, random or fixed). When a round ends the top three stand on the podium and the next round starts right away.

Choose between jelly, jelly bear and pudding; your color is one of eight, given by join order. Send emotes with Z, X, C and V (wave, taunt, cheer, sad). Available in English and Korean.

Good to know
- Online only. An internet connection is required, and there is no single-player practice mode or computer opponents. Rounds are played by whoever is on the game server.
- Played with the keyboard (mouse for menus). Gamepads are not supported.
- No accounts, payments, ads or chat. Your player name is visible to others in the room, so do not use your real name.

Controls: Left/Right or A/D move, Up/W/Space jump, Shift dash, Down crouch, Down + jump drop through a platform, Z/X/C/V emotes, Esc settings.
